Views

Short positions on questions clients ask me about. I keep these updated as the landscape moves.

AI on the shop floor adds two new attack surfaces

Predictive maintenance models create a new outbound path for production data and a new input that attackers can poison. Operator copilots add another: a maintenance manual or ticket containing hidden instructions can steer what the assistant recommends.

Treat model inputs as untrusted, keep a human approving any action on the line, and assess AI use cases against the NIST AI RMF before they reach production.

Why IT patching rules fail in a plant

A 30 day patch target makes sense for laptops. On a validated filling line it can mean an unplanned stoppage, a requalification and lost output worth more than the risk.

Plants need risk based treatment instead: compensating controls now, patching aligned to the production calendar, and exploitation evidence such as the KEV list deciding what jumps the queue.

Writing a cyber proposal a finance director will read

Start with the business event the client fears, such as a lost retail contract or a recall, and put a number on it. Show phases with clear exit points so the client is never committed to more than they can see value in.

Agree how benefits will be measured before the work starts. It turns the final report from a list of activities into evidence for the next investment.
